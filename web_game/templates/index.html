⚔️ THE LOST KINGDOM ⚔️

A Text-Based RPG Adventure

How to Play

⚔️ Combat

Fight enemies in turn-based combat. Attack, use items, or flee!

🗺️ Exploration

Travel between locations, talk to NPCs, and discover secrets.

📦 Inventory

Collect weapons, armor, and potions. Equip items to boost your stats.

🎯 Quests

Complete the main quest by defeating the Dragon!

🏪 Shopping

Buy and sell items at the General Store.
